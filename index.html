A California company, built around sound

Family-owned and independent since 1979, Renkus-Heinz designs and builds professional loudspeakers in Southern California.

Who we are

We put sound exactly where a room needs it, in international airports, landmark museums, corporate atriums, cathedrals, auditoriums and lecture halls, and hundreds of demanding spaces in between. It’s work we’ve become a world leader in, one difficult space at a time.

What we stand for

Customer first

The most important person in the room is always the audience. We build for the moment someone hears it.

Integrity & accountability

What we say we'll do, we do — and we own the result.

The room next door stays quiet

Sound reaches the people it was meant for and leaves the rest of the building alone — a concourse that stays calm, a neighbouring gallery that stays quiet, a service that never carries into the street.

To raise the benchmark for audio performance and build the best loudspeakers we can — and to keep leading with the technology, and the support, to match.

A California Identity

There's a little California sun in the sound that leaves Foothill Ranch — design, acoustics, electronics and assembly kept under one roof in Orange County. It's a way of building we take further than most; the thinking behind it is worth its own look.
