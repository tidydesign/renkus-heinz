A California company, built around sound

Family-owned and independent since 1979, Renkus-Heinz designs and builds professional loudspeakers in Southern California.

Who we are

We put sound exactly where a room needs it, in international airports, landmark museums, corporate atriums, cathedrals, auditoriums and lecture halls, and hundreds of demanding spaces in between. It’s work we’ve become a world leader in, one difficult space at a time.
